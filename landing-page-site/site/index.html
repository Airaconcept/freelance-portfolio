Frequently asked questions

Is this a real product?

No. Ledgerly is a fictional product used to demonstrate a landing page template. All names, numbers and prices are placeholders.

Does the contact form send anything?

Not in this demo. The form validates the fields in the browser and shows a confirmation. The README explains how to connect it to a form service or your own endpoint.

Which browsers are supported?

All current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on desktop and mobile. The page uses no frameworks and loads no external scripts or fonts.
